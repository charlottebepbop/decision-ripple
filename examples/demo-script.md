# Demo shot list

Film `./scripts/demo.sh`. It runs on a throwaway copy of `vault/`, so the take can be repeated and the committed notes stay as they are. Keep it under two minutes. No login.

Pause on each banner. The spoken line is the one sentence for that beat.

## 1. What is already broken

Spoken: A fresh clone already knows which assumptions have failed, and which live decisions still rest on them.

On screen: “Scan coverage was 88% of trips in October” breaks `ops-data-complete` under four live decisions, three of them critical. “Supplier flags a 3-week slip on e-cargo bikes” weakens the February delivery under “Lease half the e-cargo bikes, buy half.”

## 2. One memo, two assumptions

Spoken: One board memo can hit two assumptions, and the brief names who has to hear about both.

On screen: the `BREAKS` section for the Q1 budget (five live decisions, three critical) and the `WEAKENS` section for the bike supplier (the lease, critically). One tell list. Priya Raman is first, four critical, because the lease is critical for her on top of the budget.

## 3. The hub decision, replaced twice

Spoken: Replacing a decision twice still lands on the one that is live. The pre-check does not stop at the middle.

On screen: the first pre-check points “Open the Rotterdam South hub in Q1” at “Open the Rotterdam South hub in Q2.” The second points both the Q1 and the Q2 decisions at “Open the Rotterdam South hub in Q3.”

## 4. Why the mapping contract changed

Spoken: Ask why in the notes, and the graph says what replaced it.

On screen: “Sign an annual mapping API contract” is superseded by “Pay the mapping API monthly until the optimizer ships.”
